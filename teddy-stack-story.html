Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order? +
We recommend placing your order at least 2 days in advance. Slots may fill up before then, so booking early is always the best idea. Need something sooner? Message us and we'll do our best to help.
Can I personalize my order? +
Absolutely! Add a hat, a heart tag with your own message, or upgrade to a Custom Banner for a longer note. You can also send it directly to someone else, just add their delivery details at checkout.
What flavors and sizes are available? +
Brown Butter, Double Choco, or Mix, in stacks of 5 to 24 teddies. See the size and flavor photos above, or message us for a custom quote above 24 pcs.
How can I receive my order? +
You can choose self pickup at our Gilimanuk or Batuceper locations, or delivery using GoSend, GrabExpress, or Lalamove. We're also happy to help arrange a Lalamove for you.
