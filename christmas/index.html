FAQ

good to Know

How do I pay?

Full payment upfront. Once it's paid, your slot is all yours 🤍 Please note, paid orders can't be refunded.

When should I order?

The sooner, the safer! Our daily quota is limited, so it's first pay, first served. Ordering 25+ packs? Give us a heads-up at least 4 days before (˶ˆᗜˆ˵)

I need it soon or even today. Can I?

Just chat us on WhatsApp. If there's still a slot, we'll do our best to make it happen 🥹

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in Jakarta and Tangerang, for now.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is on you, but we can help book a Lalamove for you. Just chat us~

Can I pick up my order?

Of course! Come by Celebrar Madeleine & Cookies, Daan Mogot, Kalideres, West Jakarta (daily 11.00–18.00) or TritonVille, Batuceper, Tangerang (weekdays 09.30–16.30). Please let us know at least a day before.

Can you send to many addresses?

Yes! Just send us the address list at least 3 days before delivery.

Can I add a greeting card?

Yes, write your own message and we'll tuck it in. Sometimes a few words are what they remember most 🧸

Is it gluten-free?

We don't use any gluten ingredients. Our kitchen is shared with gluten products though, so every tool and surface is sanitized before each production. A gentler pick if you're simply avoiding gluten day to day.

Bayarnya gimana?

Full payment di awal yaa. Begitu lunas, slot langsung aman 🤍 Pesanan yang sudah dibayar nggak bisa di-refund.

Kapan sebaiknya pesan?

Makin cepat, makin aman! Kuota kami per hari terbatas, jadi first pay, first served. Kalau mau pesan 25+ pack, kindly inform us 4 days in advance (˶ˆᗜˆ˵)

Butuh cepat, atau bahkan hari ini. Bisa?

Langsung chat kami di WhatsApp aja. Kalau slotnya masih ada, we'll do our best to make it happen 🥹

Bisa kirim ke mana aja?

Untuk sekarang kami kirim ke Jakarta dan Tangerang.

Ongkirnya berapa?

Ongkir ditanggung pembeli. Tapi kami bisa bantu pesankan Lalamove. Please contact us~

Bisa ambil sendiri?

Bisa banget! Ambil di Celebrar Madeleine & Cookies, Daan Mogot, Kalideres, Jakarta Barat (setiap hari 11.00–18.00) atau TritonVille, Batuceper, Tangerang (Senin–Jumat 09.30–16.30). Kindly let us know at least H-1.

Bisa kirim ke banyak alamat?

Bisa! Kirim aja daftar alamatnya minimal 3 hari sebelum pengiriman.

Bisa tambah kartu ucapan?

Bisa dong! Tulis pesanmu, nanti kami selipkan. Because sometimes a few words are what they remember most 🧸

Apakah gluten-free?

Kami nggak pakai bahan yang mengandung gluten. Tapi karena dapur kami juga dipakai untuk produk bergluten, semua alat dan permukaan selalu kami sanitasi sebelum produksi. Jadi lebih cocok buat kamu yang sekadar mau mengurangi gluten sehari-hari.
